Selfhost Hub is a directory of self-hosted services and tools, dedicated to collecting and showcasing high-quality self-hosted software. Our goal is to help you discover, explore, and deploy self-hosted solutions that fit your needs.

## Why Self-Host?

Self-hosting means you are in full control of your data and infrastructure. Compared with third-party services, self-hosting offers:

- **Data privacy**: Your data stays entirely under your control and is never shared with third parties
- **Customization**: Freely customize and extend features to fit your needs
- **Cost control**: Often more economical than subscriptions in the long run
- **Learning opportunities**: Grow your technical skills by deploying and maintaining services
- **Community support**: Active open-source communities provide ongoing support and updates

## Features

- **Curated services**: Every listed service is carefully selected and verified
- **Detailed documentation**: Complete deployment guides and usage instructions
- **Categories & tags**: Quickly find what you need through categories and tags
- **Continuous updates**: Service information is updated and new services are added regularly
- **Open and transparent**: This site is fully open source, and community contributions are welcome
